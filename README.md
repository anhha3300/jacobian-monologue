## 🤔 What Does This App Do?

**jacobian-monologue** is a demonstration tool that answers a fascinating question: **Can changing how an AI stores or accesses its memory change what the AI actually decides?**

## ✨ Why This Matters (For the Curious)

This isn't just a toy — it's a serious research tool. In the world of artificial intelligence, people are working hard to understand how AI systems "think." One of the biggest questions is whether AI memory works like human memory: does what we remember (and what we forget) change what we do?

**jacobian-monologue** lets you see this happening in real time. It uses a cutting-edge framework called **USD** (Universal Scene Description) to structure how AI memories are organized. By tweaking a single "relation" (a connection between memories), you can flip the AI's final decision — demonstrating that memory isn't just storage; it's a living, active part of decision-making.

This kind of research helps make AI safer and more reliable. If we know exactly which memories influence an AI's choices, we can better control and audit AI behavior.

## 📚 Background on the Technology (Light Overview)

- **Language Models (LLMs)**: These are AI systems trained on vast amounts of text. They can answer questions, write stories, and solve problems.
- **Memory Systems**: A way for the AI to store and retrieve information during a conversation or task.
- **Jacobian**: A mathematical concept that measures how much a small change in one thing affects another. Here, it's used to measure how sensitive the AI's decision is to memory changes.
